Lagos Business Trip: Sample Itinerary

A sample itinerary I prepared for a fictional CEO. It covers flights, hotel, ground transport, meetings and backup plans, in the format an executive can read on a phone. All names, numbers and bookings are invented.

TravellerAmara Okoye, CEO (fictional)
RouteNairobi (NBO) to Lagos (LOS)
DatesSun 14 to Wed 17 March 2027
Booked throughSkyline Travel (fictional)

Time zones: Nairobi is EAT (UTC+3). Lagos is WAT (UTC+1), two hours behind Nairobi. All times below show the local time at that place.

Before she travels

Documents and health

  • Passport valid for at least six months after the trip
  • Visa requirements checked for her passport
  • Health entry requirements checked on official sources
  • Travel insurance confirmed

Money and tech

  • Some cash in local currency plus a travel card
  • Phone roaming or local SIM arranged
  • Power adapter and chargers packed
  • Itinerary saved offline on her phone

Work prep

  • Briefing pack on each meeting emailed on Sunday
  • Meetings added to her calendar with the Lagos time zone
  • Nairobi calls moved or covered while she is away
  • Out-of-office message set

Backup plans

If this happensI would
The outbound flight is delayed or cancelledCall Skyline Travel for the next flight, tell the driver and hotel the new arrival time, and move Monday’s 10:00 meeting to the afternoon if needed.
The driver does not appear at the airportCall the driver, then the hotel for a car. Keep a second transport option saved in advance.
The hotel has a problem with the roomAsk the hotel for an upgrade or a nearby alternative, and keep a second hotel shortlisted.
A meeting is cancelled or movedUpdate the calendar and this itinerary, tell the driver, and fill the gap with the buffer time.
Her passport or bag is lostContact the nearest embassy or consulate and the airline, and use the saved scans of her documents.
